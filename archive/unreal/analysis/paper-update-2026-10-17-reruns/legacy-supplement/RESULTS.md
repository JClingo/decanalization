# Legacy supplementary traversal-rate results

These archived files were recorded before the reward and dispersion fixes. They are supporting sensitivity evidence, not additional corrected primary conditions. Counts 4, 6, and 7 were confirmed by the author. The comparison below uses the legacy primary HRL-CDR reference and identical measurement definitions; no schema-2 primary values enter this analysis.

| Count label | Post signed mean h | Post legacy reward | Reward change vs legacy reference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| reference | 8.597 | 32.675 | 0.00% |
| 4 | 13.011 | 29.424 | -9.95% |
| 6 | 16.386 | 27.891 | -14.64% |
| 7 | 19.673 | 27.761 | -15.04% |

Increasing counted visits was associated with lower legacy post-phase reward and signed mean h farther above zero. This is consistent with an adverse effect in these recorded outcomes, but signed mean h is not MAE, and the archived dispersion field is not population SD. Do not claim a demonstrated deterioration in time-weighted regulation or compare these reward totals directly with corrected primary rewards.

The accompanying contrasts use two-sided Welch tests and unadjusted 95% intervals, with Holm correction across six exploratory comparisons.
