# Decisions and remaining rerun work

The author resolved the six methodological questions on 17 October 2026. The implementation and proposed Methods text now follow these decisions. Historical inputs and their descriptive analysis remain frozen; they cannot be converted into actual objective rewards from phase aggregates alone.

1. **Timing resolved.** Use dt = 0.05 s and 2,000 decisions = 100 s per phase (300 s per trial). The old duration field of 1000 was a constant, not elapsed seconds. Schema 2 records and verifies 100 s.
2. **Reward resolved; reruns required.** Evaluation now gathers the actual HRL/NF reward callback once per decision. Both legacy reward accumulators are disconnected. Scoring continues throughout each fixed-length phase even when |h| exceeds 100; training's terminal penalty and cutoff remain training-only. NF phase resets receive no artificial progress credit. Archived reward totals remain legacy evaluation scores.
3. **Standard deviation fixed; reruns required.** Both Blueprint standard-deviation helpers now calculate sqrt(sum((h-mean(h))²)/N). The logger records N and independently calculates population SD, mean absolute deviation from zero, RMS deviation from zero, and fraction of recorded h samples within ±5. These use the existing event/update history, not a uniformly timed trajectory: the fraction must not be described as fraction of time. Old phase summaries lack the information needed for exact retrospective correction.
4. **Provenance implemented.** Reruns produce schema-2 YAML plus a metrics JSON and provenance directory: actual loaded snapshot copies/hashes, asset and logger hashes, runtime settings, effective selected training configuration, phase-start tile/resource/agent state, policy memory, and initial/current RNG state. Scene state is recorded at the first scoring decision. These records support future matching checks; they do not establish historical pairing or guarantee bitwise replay of all engine state.
5. **Selection resolved.** Snapshots were selected by performance relative to other snapshots: HRL for mean h closer to zero and highest mean reward; NF for highest mean reward. The selected triplets are HRL 10000 and NF 5000. The NF manifest and proposed appendix use effective NFStable1 settings after overrides. This is an author-confirmed selection account, not a reconstructed candidate ranking.
6. **Scope resolved.** Keep the paper focused on the selected policies and designated datasets. Briefly state that repeated evaluation trials are not independent training replicates. No additional training-seed study is required for this paper; algorithm-wide causal claims remain outside its scope.

Do not combine schema-1 and schema-2 reward or dispersion measures. The short engineering smoke tests are validation only, not replacement paper data. Supplementary traversal counts remain 4, 6, and 7 and are supporting sensitivity evidence; their historical reward scores must remain labeled as such unless rerun.
