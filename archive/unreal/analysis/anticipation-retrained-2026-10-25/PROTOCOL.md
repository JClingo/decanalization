# Corrected anticipation retraining

Fresh HRL and NF runs and lower-learning-rate refinement candidates use the corrected BP_Agent anticipation asset. Historical models, original asset, original trial results, and import settings are preserved in the baseline archives and manifests.

The original reward functions and PPO family settings are retained. HRL is selected for the lowest mean absolute homeostatic state; NF for the highest actual received reward. Training uses environment seed 1 and initialization seed 1234. Held-out selection uses eight CCC trials at seed 20001 per checkpoint, after the first gather and every 25 gathers (250,000 transitions). Leading fresh and refined candidates and the preserved winners are then compared on 32 CCC trials at independent validation seed 30001, with duplicate network sets evaluated once. The final test uses seed 10001, excluded from both selection stages, and 250 trials per family and CDR/CCC condition. Each trial contains three 100-second phases at 20 decisions/second. Trial metrics retain the existing event-sampled definition of absolute homeostatic state.

The initial plan used minimums of 320 HRL and 640 NF gathers. After five HRL validation regressions and falling training return, the HRL minimum was revised to 200 gathers and its best checkpoint was restored into a separate lower-rate refinement. NF fresh training retains its 640-gather minimum. This revision follows measured objective performance rather than continuing a deteriorated HRL run to match an update count. Each run stops after four validation checkpoints without a 0.5% material improvement once that minimum is reached. The best observed validation checkpoint is retained, including smaller improvements. A 2,000-gather process ceiling and 16-hour engine guard prevent unbounded execution; reaching a guard must be reported as a limit rather than convergence. Refinements start from the best fresh HRL checkpoint and preserved historical NF winner. Both use policy learning rate 1e-5 and critic learning rate 1e-4, a 100-gather minimum and 500-gather ceiling. All four initial network hashes are verified by Unreal and again by the trainer. The best observed fresh or refined candidate is selected for each family before final testing. HRL refinement may stop after the 100-gather minimum if five validations fail to beat its starting reference; recovery from an initially worse refinement checkpoint is not treated as improvement beyond that reference. The fresh NF run ended with a native socket communication timeout after 538 gathers / 5.38 million transitions, before its originally planned 640-gather minimum. Its best checkpoint was retained, and four completed validations after that checkpoint had shown no improvement. The failure receipts and all models remain preserved; this interruption is not relabeled a successful training completion. Independent candidate confirmation proceeds from the preserved models, as recorded in training_closeout.json. This is an optimization protocol, not a guarantee of a globally optimal policy. One initialization seed per family limits conclusions about training variability.

Unreal 5.5 on this Mac lacks the shared-memory implementation used by the original project. Isolated project copies use the engine's localhost socket transport with a trainer-import readiness handshake, CPU execution, and a separate Python environment. Both native smoke tests updated freshly initialized networks. Native evaluation must load matching snapshot hashes and complete all requested phases and receipt checks before a checkpoint is scored.

Four snapshots per checkpoint, effective trainer settings, trial sidecars, completion receipts, selection history, logs and hashes are retained. Main-project inference defaults and historical data are preserved. Selected models and final comparison artifacts are exported to analysis/anticipation-retrained-2026-10-25 only after successful final evaluation.
